隐私说明

匣子（Xiazi）是装在你自己电脑上的 Windows 软件。

不收集数据

匣子不收集任何数据。

没有账号，没有广告，也不统计你怎么使用。

内容留在你的电脑上

照片、视频、密码和关键照片，都放在你自己选定的文件夹里。

匣子不会把这些内容发送出去。

软件本身不联网

匣子自己不会连接网络。

「赞助作者」只会用系统自带的浏览器打开第三方赞助页面：

  • 爱发电：https://afdian.com/a/lostidea
  • Ko-fi：https://ko-fi.com/lostidea

付钱是在这些网站上完成的。匣子不经手钱。赞助也不会解锁匣子里的任何功能。

偏好设置放在哪里

从 Microsoft Store 安装时，主题和语言保存在你的用户文件夹里。

便携版把主题和语言放在程序旁边。

保险库里的内容不在那里。

联系

有问题请发邮件：lostidea@gmail.com

Privacy

Xiazi (匣子) is a Windows app that stays on your own computer.

No data collection

Xiazi collects no data.

There is no account, no ads, and no usage statistics.

Your files stay on your computer

Photos, videos, your password, and the key photo stay in a folder you choose.

The app does not send them out.

The app does not connect to the network

Xiazi does not connect to the network itself.

Sponsor (赞助作者) only opens the system browser to these third-party sponsor pages:

  • 爱发电 (Afdian): https://afdian.com/a/lostidea
  • Ko-fi: https://ko-fi.com/lostidea

Payment happens on those sites. Xiazi does not handle money. Sponsoring unlocks nothing in the app.

Where preferences are kept

If you install from the Microsoft Store, theme and language preferences stay in your user folder.

The portable version keeps them beside the program.

Vault content is not kept there.
